A club built by players

FortGame started as a small room with 40 PCs. Today it is one of the few clubs in Astana that never closes.

Why we exist

Good gaming hardware is expensive, and playing alone at home gets old. We wanted a place where anyone in Astana can sit down at a top-tier PC, find a team and play at any hour.

Our audience is students, office workers who play after 18:00, and local esports teams who need a quiet room to train. Everything in the club, from the 240 Hz monitors to food delivery to your desk, is chosen for long, comfortable sessions.

  • Performance first. PCs are upgraded every two years, games are updated nightly.
  • Respect at the table. Toxic behaviour gets one warning, then a ban.
  • Local scene. We host free cups so new teams can get match experience.

How we grew

  1. 2022

    Opened on Imanova Street with 40 PCs and one admin per shift.

  2. 2023

    Added the VIP Room and the BootCamp for esports teams.

  3. 2024

    First FortGame Cup: 32 CS2 teams over two weekends.

  4. 2025

    Grew to 80 PCs and opened the PS5 Lounge.

  5. 2026

    Launched online booking and member accounts on this website.

Club rules

  • Bring an ID. We check it on your first visit.
  • Guests under 16 can stay until 22:00. After that, 16+ only.
  • Drinks with lids only at the desks. Meals in the lounge or with a tray.
  • Booked seats are held for 15 minutes after the start time.
  • Voice chat is fine. Shouting at the screen is for the VIP Room.
